Outside walls

What are the outside walls like?

How to tell: homes built before about 1930 usually have solid walls, with some bricks laid end-on. A neat pattern of filled drill holes in the mortar means the cavity has been insulated.

Loft

Is the loft insulated?
Does the insulation come up above the top of the wooden joists?

Joists are usually about 100 mm deep. Insulation well above them is probably a modern top-up of 200 mm or more.
